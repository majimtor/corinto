crawlers.json is derived from the list of crawler user agents of
https://github.com/monperrus/crawler-user-agents, file
crawler-user-agents.json at commit
a727b357908004d61ba5758fa6425d379d9a4828 (2026-11-12).

It keeps, for each of the 1,506 entries and in the same order, the pattern
and the tags, unchanged. The address of the page of each crawler, its
description, the date it was added and its examples are left out: the
classifier reads only the pattern and the tags.

testdata/crawler-examples.json holds the examples of the same list, which
are real user-agent strings, for the tests of this package. Of its 2,125
examples, 156 are left out because they could point at a person: the ones
with an at sign, a mailto, an account of a code hosting site or the home
page of a user. The other 1,969 are unchanged. Two patterns of the list are
themselves the mailbox of a company, which is how its crawler names itself;
they are kept, because without them that crawler would be counted as a
person.
